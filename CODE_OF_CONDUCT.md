# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want apitame to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps:

- being kind and patient, especially with newcomers;
- giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully;
- focusing on what is best for the project and its users;
- taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- harassment, insults or personal attacks, in public or in private;
- sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome advances;
- publishing others' private information without permission;
- any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report problems to the maintainer through a [private security advisory](https://github.com/albert-canfield/apitame/security/advisories/new) marked "Code of conduct", or by contacting the maintainer listed on the GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove, edit or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behavior is harmful.
